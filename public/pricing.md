# Pricing — LEVEL7

LEVEL7 has two things you can hire: growth marketing (the agency) and Bob (the AI employee product). Pricing works differently for each.

## Bob — AI Employee (self-serve, fixed pricing)

Bob is a hosted AI employee that lives in your WhatsApp or Telegram, remembers every customer, and does real work: answering leads, booking meetings, following up, and more. Sign up directly at https://bob.thelevel7.ai/.

- Trial: 7 days free, no payment due at signup
- Lite: $29/month
- Silver: $59/month
- Pro: $99/month
- Gold: $199/month

Usage on top of the plan is pay-as-you-go with a built-in monthly cap, so there is no surprise bill. Full plan details: https://docs.getbob.thelevel7.ai/account/plans-and-limits/

## Growth Marketing (agency services, custom pricing)

Services: Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram), TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, YouTube Ads, native advertising, social media marketing, SEO and search visibility, CRM automation, and web development.

These are scoped and quoted per business, based on ad spend, channels, and goals, not a fixed price list. Book a call to get a concrete plan and number: https://thelevel7.ai/contact
